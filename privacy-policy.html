Privacy Policy — Background Video Recorder

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Background Video Recorder app ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have.

Publisher: Last Ride Tech

Contact: thelastridetech@gmail.com

1. Your videos stay on your phone

  • Videos you record are saved only on your device (or on the SD card or folder you choose). We never upload them, and we cannot see them.
  • Trimmed copies made with the Video Cropper are also saved only on your device.
  • "Hide videos" moves your videos into the app's private storage on your phone. Nothing leaves the device.
  • If you uninstall the app, videos in the app's private storage are deleted by Android. Videos saved to your Movies folder, SD card or a folder you picked stay there until you delete them.

2. Settings and PIN

  • Your settings and scheduled recordings are stored only on your device.
  • If you set a PIN, only a salted, one-way hash of it is stored on your device. The PIN itself is never stored or sent anywhere. Fingerprint unlock is handled by Android; the app never receives your fingerprint.
  • Your PIN hash and hidden videos are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Other settings may be included in your own Google backup if you have backup turned on.

3. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy

MicrophoneTo record the sound of your videos — only while you record.
NotificationsTo show the recording notification with its Stop button, and schedule reminders.
Alarms & remindersSo scheduled recordings start on time.

The app does not access your location, contacts, photos or other files.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads. Ads are provided by these companies, which act as independent controllers of the data they collect:

  • Start.io — https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy-site/
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) — https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

To show and measure ads, these companies may collect from your device:

  • the advertising ID (you can reset it or delete it in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads),
  • device information (model, Android version, language, screen size), the app's name and version,
  • IP address (from which an approximate, country/city-level location may be inferred),
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and crash/diagnostic data about the ads.

They may use this information to show ads, to personalise ads (only where you have agreed, where the law requires consent), to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud.

Your choices

  • Before any ad is loaded, the app asks once whether you allow personalised ads or only non-personalised ads. Your choice is passed to Start.io and Unity Ads, and you can change it any time in the app's Settings → "Personalised ads".
  • You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID").
  • "Watch a video to remove ads" in the menu: each fully watched video gives 2 hours without ads, up to 24 hours.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Ads in the app are not child-directed.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data on our own servers — the app has no account and no server of its own. The only data shared is what the ad companies listed in section 4 collect directly through their software in the app.

7. Security

Your videos, settings and PIN hash stay on your device and are protected by Android's app sandbox. The PIN screen cannot be captured in screenshots and is blank in the recent-apps preview.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold any personal data ourselves, please contact the ad companies listed in section 4 for data they collect, or write to us at thelastridetech@gmail.com and we will help.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
