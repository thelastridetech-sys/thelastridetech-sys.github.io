Background Video Recorder

An Android app that records video with your phone's camera while the screen is off or while you use other apps. Tap record, lock your phone, and stop whenever you like from the notification. Android always shows a recording notification, so you always know when the camera is in use.

  • Back or front camera, quality of your choice, zoom and flashlight
  • Scheduled recordings, time and file-size limits
  • Save to the phone, an SD card or a folder you choose; hide videos from the gallery
  • PIN or fingerprint lock, built-in gallery and video trimmer
  • Your videos stay on your phone; the app never uploads them

Free, supported by ads. Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
